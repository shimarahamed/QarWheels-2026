## Scope and evidence
The patterns are original, operational syntheses informed by the public references in `sources.md` and professional UX conventions; they are not verbatim transcriptions, and the source organizations do not endorse this catalog. Video timings are examples, not standards.
Several rules may legitimately conflict (e.g., density versus spaciousness); resolve by user tasks, accessibility, product conventions, and validation.
