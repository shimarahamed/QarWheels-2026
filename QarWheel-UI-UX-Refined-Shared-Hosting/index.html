GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
worth knowing.

What is QarWheel?

QarWheel is a Qatar-focused automotive technology experience that brings vehicle information, AI-assisted guidance, garage discovery, repair updates and recorded vehicle history together.

Does AI replace a mechanic?

No. AI guidance is informational. Vehicle problems should be assessed and repaired by qualified professionals.

Can I download the app now?

Official App Store and Google Play download destinations have not yet been confirmed. We will display the verified links when they become available.

Can garages become partners?

Yes, garage partnership interest is part of QarWheel’s plan. Visit the For Garages page for an overview. Official enquiry channels are being finalized.

Can I use QarWheel in Arabic?

This website supports English and Arabic. Availability of languages inside the mobile application should be confirmed in the app itself.
