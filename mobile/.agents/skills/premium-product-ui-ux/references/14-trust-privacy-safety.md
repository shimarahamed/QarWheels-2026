# 14 — Trust, privacy and safe decisions

**Use for:** trust. Each rule is a context-sensitive heuristic unless a formal standard is explicitly named; do not apply mechanically.

### UX-131 · State data use plainly

- **Apply:** Explain why personal information is needed at the point of collection.
- **Verify:** Users can make an informed choice.

### UX-132 · Default to least privilege

- **Apply:** Request only necessary permissions and avoid unnecessary sharing by default.
- **Verify:** Sensitive capabilities are restricted.

### UX-133 · Make consequences explicit

- **Apply:** Explain irreversible actions, audience visibility, and external effects before final confirmation.
- **Verify:** The user understands who or what is affected.

### UX-134 · Avoid dark patterns

- **Apply:** Do not use disguised ads, hidden opt-outs, forced continuity, or shame-based copy.
- **Verify:** Rejecting an optional offer remains straightforward.

### UX-135 · Show authentic proof

- **Apply:** Use real reviews, verified security claims, and authentic testimonials only.
- **Verify:** Trust badges are backed by evidence.

### UX-136 · Protect sensitive content

- **Apply:** Mask or omit secrets in UI, logs, screenshots, examples, and clipboard affordances.
- **Verify:** Test fixtures contain no real credentials.

### UX-137 · Confirm destructive scope

- **Apply:** Name affected records and permanence; favor undo when safe.
- **Verify:** Users cannot easily delete the wrong item unnoticed.

### UX-138 · Distinguish authority levels

- **Apply:** Make read-only, admin-only, external, and destructive features clear in UI.
- **Verify:** Unauthorized actions do not appear as false promises.

### UX-139 · Avoid misleading defaults

- **Apply:** Never preselect paid extras, consent, or risky actions without an appropriate user choice.
- **Verify:** Default choices do not exploit inattention.

### UX-140 · Design trustworthy AI states

- **Apply:** Distinguish generated suggestions from verified facts, expose uncertainty and human override where needed.
- **Verify:** Users know what is automated and can correct it.
