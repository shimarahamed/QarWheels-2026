# 05 — Visual hierarchy, color and surfaces

**Use for:** visual. Each rule is a context-sensitive heuristic unless a formal standard is explicitly named; do not apply mechanically.

### UX-041 · Establish one focal point

- **Apply:** Make the primary action and information dominant through scale, contrast, or placement.
- **Verify:** First glance indicates what matters.

### UX-042 · Use contrast with purpose

- **Apply:** Make contrast express hierarchy, action availability, and important status, not just decoration.
- **Verify:** Essential elements remain discernible.

### UX-043 · Reserve accent colors

- **Apply:** Apply brand accents to the most meaningful interaction and status surfaces.
- **Verify:** Every element does not compete for attention.

### UX-044 · Use semantic status tokens

- **Apply:** Separate success, warning, danger, info, and neutral states from raw palette values.
- **Verify:** Status changes remain coherent across themes.

### UX-045 · Avoid color-only meaning

- **Apply:** Pair colored indicators with labels, patterns, icons, or programmatic meaning.
- **Verify:** Meaning survives grayscale and assistive use.

### UX-046 · Build light and dark themes

- **Apply:** Test contrast and elevation independently in each theme instead of inverting colors mechanically.
- **Verify:** Neither theme hides boundaries or text.

### UX-047 · Use elevation sparingly

- **Apply:** Apply shadows, borders, and layering to show interaction and containment.
- **Verify:** Surface hierarchy remains readable without deep shadows.

### UX-048 · Align optical details

- **Apply:** Fine-tune icon and label centering, baseline, and stroke weight while preserving semantic alignment.
- **Verify:** Repeated rows and controls look visually coherent.

### UX-049 · Maintain icon semantics

- **Apply:** Use familiar icons with labels for ambiguous actions; keep an icon family consistent.
- **Verify:** Icons do not require guessing.

### UX-050 · Favor brand restraint

- **Apply:** Apply gradients, blur, and glow only when they enhance identity without hiding controls.
- **Verify:** Reduced effects retain a professional interface.
