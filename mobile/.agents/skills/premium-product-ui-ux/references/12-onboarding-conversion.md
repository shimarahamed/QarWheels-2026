# 12 — Onboarding, conversion and engagement

**Use for:** conversion. Each rule is a context-sensitive heuristic unless a formal standard is explicitly named; do not apply mechanically.

### UX-111 · Demonstrate value early

- **Apply:** Help users perform the first meaningful task before requesting optional setup.
- **Verify:** Time to first value is measurable.

### UX-112 · Ask permission in context

- **Apply:** Request notifications, location, contacts, or camera access only when the user understands the benefit.
- **Verify:** Permissions are not demanded on first launch without explanation.

### UX-113 · Allow exploration where safe

- **Apply:** Offer guest, preview, or sample content when account creation is not technically essential.
- **Verify:** Prospects can understand the product before committing.

### UX-114 · Make pricing transparent

- **Apply:** State recurring charges, trials, renewal conditions, and cancellation paths before purchase.
- **Verify:** No material fees are hidden behind the final step.

### UX-115 · Minimize checkout friction

- **Apply:** Prefer straightforward fields and guest checkout where supported; do not force unnecessary account creation.
- **Verify:** Purchase can complete with minimal interruptions.

### UX-116 · Provide a clear primary CTA

- **Apply:** Give one obvious primary next step per decision surface while retaining honest alternatives.
- **Verify:** Users know what clicking will do.

### UX-117 · Avoid deceptive urgency

- **Apply:** Do not fabricate countdowns, inventory scarcity, or social proof.
- **Verify:** Every urgency claim has verifiable underlying data.

### UX-118 · Show onboarding progress

- **Apply:** Use clear checkpoints, skip options for optional tasks, and realistic completion expectations.
- **Verify:** Users can recover or return later.

### UX-119 · Confirm next steps

- **Apply:** After signup or payment, show status, receipt or reference, and what happens next.
- **Verify:** Users do not need to guess if the action succeeded.

### UX-120 · Measure ethical outcomes

- **Apply:** Track completion, comprehension, and satisfaction with conversion, not clicks alone.
- **Verify:** Improvement does not rely on dark patterns.
