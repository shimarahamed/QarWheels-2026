Before your next journey.

What does QarWheel connect?

QarWheel brings symptom guidance, garage discovery, repair communication and recorded vehicle history into one car-care experience. The app is the central product; this website introduces how it works.

Can AI give me a guaranteed diagnosis?

No. Guidance can help explain the information you provide and prepare questions for a garage. It cannot guarantee a diagnosis or replace inspection by a qualified technician.

What should I discuss with a garage?

Describe the symptoms, relevant vehicle details and the service you need. Confirm the scope of work, pricing and availability directly with the service provider.

How does repair tracking work?

The illustrated journey covers vehicle receipt, inspection, diagnosis, approval, repair, quality check and readiness. Updates depend on the information recorded by the service provider.

What is a vehicle passport?

An ongoing record of key vehicle information and recorded service, maintenance and repair events. Its completeness depends on the information added to it.

How can garages take part?

The For Garages page introduces the partner experience: helping drivers discover relevant services and keeping service communication clearer. Official contact details and partnership terms remain to be confirmed.
