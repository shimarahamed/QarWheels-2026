# QarWheel: Intelligent Car Management MVP Launch Guide

Welcome to **QarWheel**, the next-generation platform designed to bridge the gap between car owners and service providers in Qatar. This document outlines the core features, functions, and the "intelligent" edge of our MVP.

---

## 🚗 What is QarWheel?
QarWheel is an all-in-one automotive ecosystem. It simplifies vehicle maintenance for owners through AI-driven insights and provides vendors with a robust digital storefront and management suite.

---

## 👤 For Car Owners: Your Digital Garage

### 1. Intelligent Dashboard
A centralized hub where you can see your vehicle health, upcoming appointments, and personalized maintenance alerts.

### 2. "My Cars" Management
*   **Virtual Garage**: Register multiple vehicles.
*   **AI VIN Decoding**: Simply enter your VIN, and our AI automatically pulls your vehicle's make, model, engine specs, and trim level.
*   **Digital Logs**: Keep all your car details in one secure place.

### 3. Smart Service Booking
*   **Garage Discovery**: Browse high-rated service centers near you using our interactive map.
*   **Instant Booking**: Select services (Oil change, Brake repair, etc.) and pick a time slot directly from the app.
*   **Real-time Status**: Track your car's progress while it's at the garage.

### 4. AI-Powered Insights
*   **Predictive Maintenance**: Based on your car's age and mileage, QarWheel predicts when you’ll need your next service before issues arise.
*   **Service Summaries**: Our AI analyzes long service histories and gives you a simple "Health Score" and summary, so you know exactly what’s been done.

---

## 🏪 For Vendors: Business Growth Reimagined

### 1. Vendor Command Center
A powerful dashboard providing a 360-degree view of your garage operations.

### 2. Booking & Staff Management
*   **Digital Calendar**: Manage all incoming appointments with a drag-and-drop interface.
*   **Staff Allocation**: Assign mechanics to specific jobs and track their efficiency.

### 3. Inventory & Services
*   **Dynamic Menu**: Update your service list and pricing in real-time.
*   **Parts Tracking**: Keep track of your inventory levels and get alerts when stock is low.

### 4. Advanced Analytics
*   **Revenue Insights**: View daily, weekly, and monthly financial reports.
*   **Customer Trends**: Understand your most popular services and peak booking times.
*   **Marketing Tools**: Create promotions and discounts to attract more customers during slow periods.

## 🧠 How the "Intelligent" Functions Work

| Feature | How it Works | Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI VIN Search** | Uses Genkit and Gemini to parse manufacturer databases. | No manual data entry; 100% accuracy on specs. |
| **Predictive Alerts** | Machine learning models analyze vehicle patterns. | Prevents costly breakdowns and increases car lifespan. |
| **Map Integration** | Uses Leaflet for real-time geographic searching. | Find the closest and best-rated help instantly. |
